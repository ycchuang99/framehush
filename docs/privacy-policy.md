# Framehush Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

Last updated / 最後更新：2026-10-20

## English

Framehush reduces background noise in speech played by a Chrome tab. Audio processing runs on your device.

### Information accessed and its purpose

When you choose **Enable denoising**, Framehush accesses the audio playing in the selected tab. This audio is website content and may contain speech or personal information. It is used only to reduce background noise and play the processed audio back to you. Framehush does not access your microphone or camera.

Framehush also uses the selected tab's numeric identifier and temporary session status to manage playback. It uses Chrome's interface language to choose the extension's display language. It does not read page text, browsing history, cookies, account details, or the tab's URL or title.

### Processing, retention, and sharing

The audio is processed in short, temporary memory buffers. Framehush does not make audio recordings, save audio files, transcribe speech, or upload audio. When you stop noise reduction, the extension stops capturing the tab and releases its audio-processing resources. Session status and error messages exist only in memory; Framehush does not keep a usage history.

The extension has no analytics, advertising, tracking, or remote audio service. Its noise-reduction model and executable code are bundled with the extension. The developer does not receive or have access to your playback audio, and the extension does not sell, share, or transfer that audio to others. It does not use your audio to train models.

### Your control

Audio processing starts only after you enable it. Use **Stop denoising** to stop capture. Closing the popup alone does not stop processing. Closing the captured tab or disabling or uninstalling the extension also ends capture. There are no saved audio recordings or extension account records to delete.

### Permissions

- **activeTab:** allows the extension to work with the tab you select through the extension's action.
- **tabCapture:** captures that tab's audio after you enable noise reduction.
- **offscreen:** runs the local audio processor while the popup is closed.

Framehush's use of data is limited to its noise-reduction feature and complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data is not used for advertising, creditworthiness, or lending decisions.

### Contact and changes

For questions, open an issue at [Framehush on GitHub](https://github.com/ycchuang99/framehush/issues). Do not include private recordings or sensitive personal information in a public issue. Information you choose to submit to GitHub is handled through GitHub under its own policies, separately from the extension's audio processing.

This policy will be updated if the extension's data practices change.

## 繁體中文

Framehush 用於降低 Chrome 分頁播放人聲時的背景雜音。所有音訊處理都在你的裝置上進行。

### 存取的資訊與用途

你按下「啟用降噪」後，Framehush 會存取所選分頁正在播放的音訊。這些音訊屬於網站內容，可能包含談話或個人資訊，只用於降低背景雜音並播放處理後的聲音。Framehush 不會存取麥克風或相機。

Framehush 也會使用所選分頁的數字識別碼與暫時的處理狀態來管理播放，並依 Chrome 的介面語言選擇顯示語言。不讀取網頁文字、瀏覽紀錄、Cookie、帳戶資料、分頁網址或標題。

### 處理、保留與分享

音訊以短暫的記憶體緩衝進行處理。Framehush 不會錄製音訊、儲存音訊檔案、將語音轉成文字或上傳音訊。停止降噪後，擴充功能會停止擷取分頁音訊並釋放處理資源。處理狀態與錯誤訊息僅存在記憶體中，不保留使用紀錄。

本擴充功能沒有分析追蹤、廣告、使用者追蹤或遠端音訊服務。降噪模型與執行程式碼皆包含在擴充功能中。開發者不會收到或存取你播放的音訊；擴充功能不販售、分享或傳送這些音訊給其他人，也不會使用音訊訓練模型。

### 你的控制方式

只有在你啟用降噪後才會開始處理音訊。按「停止降噪」可停止擷取；只關閉面板不會停止處理。關閉擷取中的分頁，或停用、解除安裝擴充功能，也會結束擷取。沒有已儲存的音訊錄製檔或擴充功能帳戶紀錄需要刪除。

### 權限

- **activeTab：**讓擴充功能在你點選擴充功能圖示後，使用你選擇的分頁。
- **tabCapture：**在你啟用降噪後，擷取該分頁的音訊。
- **offscreen：**在面板關閉後，繼續執行裝置上的音訊處理。

Framehush 僅為降噪功能使用資料，並遵守 Chrome 線上應用程式商店的使用者資料政策及其「限制使用」規定。資料不會用於廣告、信用評估或貸款決策。

### 聯絡與政策更新

如有問題，請至 [Framehush GitHub 問題頁](https://github.com/ycchuang99/framehush/issues)提出。請勿在公開問題中提供私人錄音或敏感個人資訊。你主動提交給 GitHub 的資訊由 GitHub 依其政策處理，與本擴充功能的音訊處理分開。

若擴充功能的資料處理方式改變，將同步更新本政策。
