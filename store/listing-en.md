# English listing

## Name

Framehush

## Short description

Reduce background noise in speech from a Chrome tab. Process audio locally without recording or uploading.

## Detailed description

Framehush reduces background noise in speech from videos and other audio playing in Chrome, helping you focus on dialogue and narration.

Play audio in a tab, open Framehush, and choose Enable denoising. Processing continues when you close the popup. Open Framehush again and choose Stop denoising when you are finished.

• Real-time speech noise reduction using RNNoise.
• Audio processing stays on your device. No audio recordings, uploads, or transcription.
• No account, analytics, or advertising.
• English, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese interface, selected from your browser language.
• Open the popup with Alt+Shift+N. You can change the shortcut in Chrome.

Designed for speech. Music and sound effects may change, and background music or overlapping voices cannot always be removed. Some audio/video delay may occur. Chrome internal pages and some protected content cannot be captured. Requires Chrome 116 or later.

Permissions:
• activeTab selects the tab you choose through the extension action.
• tabCapture captures that tab's audio after you enable processing.
• offscreen keeps the local audio processor running when the popup closes.
